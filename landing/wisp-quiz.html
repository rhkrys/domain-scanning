Is your tax office ready to show its WISP?

Twelve questions for tax preparers. Three minutes. See your readiness score, your estimated exposure in dollars, and what it costs to cover it. Confidence, not jargon.

The twelve questions

Pick the answer that is true today. Questions 1 to 10 are scored, 2 points each. Questions 11 and 12 size your estimate. "Not sure" counts as a no, because a plan needs a known answer.

1. Which email do you use for client work?
2. Where is that email backed up?
3. Do you use a second sign-in step on email and tax software?
4. Do you have a written security plan with your office's details?
5. Who is your named security lead?
6. When did you last restore a file from backup as a test?
7. Are your computers and laptops encrypted?
8. Do you have a list of every device that opens client data?
9. A laptop is stolen tonight. Who do you call first?
10. When someone leaves, how fast is their access removed?
11. How many devices open client data?
12. Approximately how many returns do you process every year?
