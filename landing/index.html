Straight answers to what people ask before they're ready to buy.

How do I know if I've been targeted by a scam?
Common signs: unexpected requests for money or login details, urgent pressure to act fast, slightly-off email addresses or phone numbers, and requests to move outside normal channels. A personal security audit checks your accounts and public footprint for the specific ways you're exposed.
What does a personal cybersecurity audit include?
A full review of your accounts, devices, and public digital footprint, plus a plain-English report of what's exposed and how to fix it. The free audit includes prioritized recommendations and a quarterly re-check so improvements stick.
How can I protect elderly parents from scams?
Give them a fast way to verify a suspicious message before acting on it, ideally a real person they can call. The VIP Families or Solopreneurs plan covers up to 5 people, including parents, with a 9–5 line for anything that looks off.
What is executive cybersecurity concierge protection?
Dedicated, 24/7 monitoring and response for executives, founders, and public figures whose visibility makes them frequent targets. It includes a named support manager, continuous account and credential monitoring, and immediate incident response, any hour.
How much does personal cybersecurity monitoring cost?
Three tiers: a free audit for individuals, $1,500/month for families and small teams (up to 5 people), and $10,000/month for executive concierge protection with 24/7 dedicated monitoring.
Is a personal cybersecurity service worth it for a small business owner?
If you're responsible for staff as well as yourself, a shared service catches what one person alone would miss, like a fake invoice or a spoofed vendor email. The VIP Families or Solopreneurs plan covers up to 5 people under one 9–5 support line.
