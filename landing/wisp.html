Questions preparers ask

I already have a free template. Is that enough?
It is a good start. The rule asks for a plan that describes your office and is kept up to date. We turn the template into that.
My tax software is secure. Why do I need this?
Your software protects its own systems. The rule names you as the one responsible for your office, your email and your devices.
I am a solo preparer. Does this apply to me?
Yes. The rule applies at any size. A solo plan is shorter and simpler.
What does "per device" mean on my bill?
Count each computer, laptop, tablet or phone that opens client tax data. Multiply by your tier's device fee. Add $29. That is your monthly total.
Do you work on Macs?
Yes. Macs and Windows.
What happens if something goes wrong?
Your plan lists who to call and in what order. On Concierge, we make those calls with you.
