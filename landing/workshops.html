Answers

Questions before you join

Who are these workshops for?
Family offices and the organizations that look like them: foundations, venture and private funds, capital markets managers, and the finance, operations, compliance and IT people who run them.
Do I need to be technical?
No. We go step by step in plain language. Confidence, not jargon.
What does it cost?
The workshops and the Risk Snapshot are free. There is no pitch during the session.
What should I bring?
Your laptop, Zoom, and the one document the session names when you register. For most sessions that is your last insurance application or your list of who has access to what.
Can my assistant or controller join with me?
Yes. People get the most out of it when the person who manages the calendar, the inbox or the books is in the room too.
I missed one. Is there a replay?
Yes. Everyone who registers gets the replay and the workbook within a day.
